Your camera roll is full of moments you never looked at twice. Echo brings one back every morning: a photo from your past, beautifully framed, for one quiet minute of remembering.

Keep it with a few words or a voice note — or just as it is. Every memory you keep joins your private memory wall.

A MEMORY A DAY
• One photo from your past every morning, chosen for variety across years and places
• Not the right one today? Swipe for another
• Only photos you took — no images saved from other apps, and no screenshots unless you turn them on
• Add a line or a voice note, now or later; with Apple Intelligence, Echo can suggest a question about the photo, right on your iPhone
• A gentle daily reminder, and a streak that grows one day at a time

YOUR PRIVATE MEMORY WALL
• Every kept memory lands on a visual wall of your past
• Revisit the places your photos were taken, on a map
• Widgets put a memory from your past on your Home Screen

RELIVE AND SHARE — IF YOU WANT TO
• Flip through your year as a real book, page by page, then export it to print
• Cinematic recap films stitch your memories together
• Share a memory as a beautiful story card — and video memories as video

ON YOUR WRIST
• Today's memory on Apple Watch: keep it with one tap, then add a voice note
• Your streak and recent memories, and a complication for your watch face
• The Apple Watch app is free

ACTUALLY PRIVATE
• Your memories stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud — Echo has no photo server
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• Anonymous usage stats and crash reports help us improve Echo — never photos, notes or names
• Place names and the map use Apple Maps; Echo sends a coordinate, never a photo
• Lock Echo with Face ID if you like

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
• iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, in portrait and landscape
• Echo speaks 38 languages, with full right-to-left support
• Dates in the Gregorian or Hijri (Umm al-Qura) calendar
