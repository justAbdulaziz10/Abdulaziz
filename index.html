Selected work

Mara

An AI-powered health and wellness platform.

What I did
Product requirements, AI workflows, APIs, QA, analytics and system integrations.
Outcome
Founded in January 2026. 250+ people on the waitlist and 38 private beta users. Completed Misk Launchpad Cohort 9. Incubated at Monsha’at.
Shipped
1,122 pull requests merged across Mara’s repositories, most of them in the Mara app, and 48 merge requests on GitLab, where the builds run.
Requirement
Show today's health target only when the backend has a valid one for that exact day. Otherwise say it's unavailable and offer one next step, never a guess.
Flow
  1. Read the profile and the local date
  2. Fingerprint the fields that change the target
  3. Fetch it through the provider and repository
  4. Accept only valid v2 data for that day
  5. Format the result for each screen
Decision
Validated backend targets only.Over daily targets computed on the phone, or the v1 contract with its legacy defaults. The backend owns the calculation, and every target shows where it came from. When a target is missing, comparisons hide and the recorded data stays visible.

Echo

A 0-to-1, privacy-first photo-memory app for iPhone.

What I did
Product development, UX, monetization, analytics and release management.
Outcome
1,231 active users in the last 28 days. Rated 4.8 on the App Store, in 37 languages, now on version 1.45.
Requirement
People with years of photos rarely look back at them. Surface one photo a day from their own library, chosen on the device. Mara's servers never receive the photos.
Flow
  1. Open Today
  2. See a photo from your camera roll
  3. Keep it
  4. Add words or a voice note, if you want
  5. Save it to the Wall
  6. The saved photo stays off Today, even after relaunch
Decision
Start from photos people already have.Over a blank journal page or an upload service. No account, and no copy on Mara's servers. Saved memories sync only through the person's own iCloud.
Result
1,231 active users in the last 28 days. In a unit test on a simulated library of over 2,000 photos, 40 picks in a row had no two from within three days.

Daleelk

An AI-powered travel platform for the Gulf.

What it does
Natural-language assistance, personalized trip planning and traveler safety.
How
The OpenAI API, given country-specific facts from Daleelk's own database. FastAPI, PostgreSQL and Firebase Auth. A King Saud University team project.
Requirement
Ask for a three-day trip and get three dated days, with the activity and cafe counts you asked for, lunch and dinner, and no overlapping times.
Flow
  1. Sign in
  2. Start a new trip
  3. Enter a destination and dates
  4. Get a day-by-day schedule
Decision
Answer itinerary questions first, then offer the planner.Over sending people straight to the planner. They get guidance before the builder opens.

Rassah

An AI packing assistant MVP for Saudi online stores.

Built
End to end in two days by a team of three, in Tuwaiq Academy's AI product programme with Startupbootcamp and Meta.
How
Gemini reads Arabic and English product descriptions, a 3D packing engine picks the cheapest carton that passes a separate safety check, and each run is logged to a monitoring dashboard with alerts. It runs on a demo store with synthetic products, not a live store.
Requirement
A small-store packer needs a safe carton even when product sizes are missing. With no usable size, ask for a measurement; never guess.
Flow
  1. Enter an order number from the demo store
  2. Use cached product facts or read them from the product text
  3. Search for the cheapest carton that fits safely
  4. Ask for a measurement, or split into up to four cartons
  5. Validate the layout
  6. Show the carton, Arabic packing steps and the 3D layout
Decision
Gemini reads the product text; code packs and checks.Over letting a language model compute and approve the layout. Geometry needs exact maths.
Result
All 258 feasible cases in a synthetic benchmark produced valid layouts. No real store data was used.

Pit Lane Hub

Live Formula 1 data, with F2 and F3 guides. Free and open source.

Inside
Live timing, standings, driver comparisons, track maps and every season from 2023 to 2026.
Why
I follow Formula 1 closely enough to build my own timing screen.
Requirement
Follow a session live: timing and positions, or the entry list when no live data is coming in.
Flow
  1. Open Live
  2. Load the session
  3. Show real positions, or the entry list
  4. Reload for positions at most ten seconds old
Decision
Show the entry list when there's no live data.Over drawing a timing grid with made-up positions.
