Founder, engineer, student.

I founded Mara, a Saudi technology company that builds thoughtful consumer apps. I lead the company and write a large share of its product code myself: I am the top contributor to both the Mara app and Echo.

Alongside the company I study Information Systems at King Saud University and follow a self-directed path into machine learning, from Harvard's CS50 to Stanford's CS230.

I care about software that respects people: Arabic and English treated as equals, privacy by default, and products that are finished before they ship.
